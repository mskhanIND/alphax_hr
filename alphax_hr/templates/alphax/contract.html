4. Terms · الشروط

Annual leave: {{ job.annual_days }} days per year, rising to at least 30 days after 5 years of service (Art. 109).

Sick leave, overtime (Art. 107: hourly wage + 50% of basic) and all other rights follow the Saudi Labor Law.

Notice period: {{ job.notice_days }} days for either party (Art. 75).

End-of-service award per Art. 84 (half a month's wage for each of the first five years, one month for each year after), and Art. 85 on resignation.

Social insurance (GOSI) and medical insurance as required by law.

Disputes are settled under the Saudi Labor Law and its regulations.
